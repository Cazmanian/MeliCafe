Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

Meli Cafe respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Meli Cafe website and how information provided through services linked to the website is handled.

1. Information We Collect

The Meli Cafe website does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the website.

However, visitors may voluntarily provide personal information through third-party services linked from the website.

The Meli Cafe catering request form may collect information needed to respond to catering requests, including:

  • Number of guests
  • Requested menu items
  • Whether a loukoumades-only catering box is requested
  • Approximate event location
  • Event date
  • Email address
  • Phone number

This information is voluntarily provided by visitors who choose to submit a catering request.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the catering request form may be used by Meli Cafe to:

  • Respond to catering inquiries
  • Determine whether Meli Cafe can accommodate an event
  • Discuss event details with the customer
  • Provide information about catering services
  • Contact the customer regarding their request

Meli Cafe does not sell or rent information submitted through the catering request form to third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Google Forms

The Meli Cafe catering request form is hosted by Google Forms. Information submitted through the form is processed and stored using Google's services.

Meli Cafe does not control Google's privacy practices, security practices, or handling of information within its services. Visitors should review Google's applicable privacy policies and terms when using the form.

4. Online Ordering

The Meli Cafe website provides a link to an external online ordering service operated by Dripos.

Customers who choose to place an order through the external ordering service may be required to provide information necessary to complete their order.

Information submitted through the external ordering service is handled according to that service's policies. Meli Cafe does not control the privacy practices or information-security practices of the external ordering platform.

5. Instagram and Embedded Content

The Meli Cafe website displays Instagram-related content through a third-party embedded service.

Third-party services involved in displaying this content may collect technical information about visitors, such as information associated with their browser, device, or interaction with the embedded content.

Meli Cafe does not control the information collected by Instagram, Meta, Elfsight, or other third-party services used to provide embedded content.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of these third-party services for additional information about their data practices.

6. Cookies and Technical Information

Meli Cafe does not intentionally use the website to collect personal information for advertising or behavioral tracking.

However, website hosting providers and third-party services may automatically process technical information when the website or embedded third-party content is accessed. This may include information such as an IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, or other technical information.

Third-party services may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

7. External Links

The Meli Cafe website contains links to external websites and services, including online ordering, Google Forms, and social media services.

Once a visitor leaves the Meli Cafe website and accesses a third-party service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Meli Cafe is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of external websites and services.

8. Information Security

Meli Cafe takes reasonable steps to protect information provided through its services. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because the catering form is operated through Google Forms, information submitted through the form is also subject to Google's security and privacy practices.

9. Children's Privacy

The Meli Cafe website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Meli Cafe does not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Meli Cafe may update this Privacy Policy when the website, services, or information practices change.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled by Meli Cafe, please contact Meli Cafe using the contact information provided on the website.
